# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes UK-based e-commerce sales data to identify revenue trends, customer behavior, and business growth opportunities.

---

## Dataset
Online Retail E-Commerce Dataset

Columns:
- InvoiceNo
- StockCode
- Description
- Quantity
- InvoiceDate
- UnitPrice
- CustomerID
- Country

Additional Column:
- Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice

---

## Data Cleaning
- Removed missing Customer IDs
- Removed negative quantities
- Removed negative prices
- Removed duplicates
- Added Revenue column

---

## KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Top Products

---

## Key Insights
- Revenue peaks during November and December
- UK is the highest-performing region
- Gift products dominate sales
- Repeat customers generate major revenue

---

## Recommendations
1. Increase festive inventory
2. Expand European marketing
3. Launch loyalty programs
4. Bundle products
